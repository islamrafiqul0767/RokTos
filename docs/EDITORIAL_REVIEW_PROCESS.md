# Editorial Review Process

1. **Source Sourcing**: Health articles and guidelines must cite published medical standards from the WHO, Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) Bangladesh, or Red Crescent.
2. **Medical Review**: Health content must be verified for factual accuracy regarding donation intervals, infectious screening, and contraindications.
3. **No Automated Publishing**: AI models may draft informational copy, but publication requires human review.
4. **Versioning & Attribution**: Each policy and educational article specifies publication date and policy version (e.g. `2026-v1`).
