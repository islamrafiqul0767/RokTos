# Content Safety & Medical Disclaimer Policy

## 1. Educational Content Standard
- All health guidance regarding blood groups, compatibility, donation intervals, and deferral periods must reflect published peer-reviewed medical consensus (World Health Organization and Bangladesh Directorate General of Health Services).
- AI-generated health advice is never published directly without human clinical review.
- Emotional coercion, guilt-tripping, or alarming graphics are strictly forbidden.

## 2. Mandatory Medical Transfusion Caveat
Every page handling blood requests or discovery must include the standardized medical caveat:
> *RoktoBondhu is an opt-in volunteer coordination directory, not a clinical provider or blood bank. Pre-transfusion screening for 5 infectious diseases (HIV, HBV, HCV, Syphilis, Malaria) and on-site laboratory cross-matching are strictly mandatory.*
