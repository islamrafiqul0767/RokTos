# RoktoBondhu — Project Overview

## Mission
RoktoBondhu (রক্তবন্ধু) addresses the humanitarian and privacy challenges of volunteer blood donor coordination in Bangladesh. By replacing informal social media phone broadcasts with a structured, consent-based, non-commercial digital platform, RoktoBondhu safeguards donor privacy while accelerating discovery for patients experiencing medical emergencies.

## Core Target Audience
1. **Patients and Families in Bangladesh**: Seeking volunteer blood donors in Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet, Rajshahi, Khulna, Barishal, Rangpur, and Mymensingh.
2. **Opt-in Volunteer Donors**: Healthy individuals aged 18-60 who wish to donate blood voluntarily without receiving unsolicited spam calls or having their phone numbers harvested.
3. **Hospital Staff & Medical Volunteers**: Coordinating transfusions at major tertiary centres (e.g. DMCH, BSMMU, CMCH, RMCH, SSMC).

## Non-Negotiable Tenets
- **Strictly Non-Commercial**: No fees, paid badges, or sponsored ranking.
- **Privacy By Design**: Complete segregation of private user records and public directory projections.
- **Clinical Humility**: The platform explicitly disclaims clinical eligibility or match compatibility; all transfusions require on-site laboratory cross-matching.
