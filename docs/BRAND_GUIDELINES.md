# ROKTOS — Brand Identity Guidelines

## 1. Brand Concept & Story
**ROKTOS** is a Bengali-first blood donor discovery and request coordination platform designed specifically for Bangladesh. The name derives from "রক্ত" (blood) infused with modern civic action, community vitality, and digital reliability.

- **Exact Product Name**: `ROKTOS`
- **Official Subtitle**: `ROKTOS — Blood Donor Network Bangladesh`
- **Bengali Descriptor**: `রক্তস — বাংলাদেশ স্বেচ্ছাসেবী রক্তদাতা নেটওয়ার্ক`
- **Bengali Tagline**: `রক্তের প্রয়োজনে, পাশে থাকুক মানুষ।`
- **English Tagline**: `Connecting people who need blood with willing community donors.`

---

## 2. Logo Mark & Symbolism
The ROKTOS identity mark brings together three complementary metaphors:
1. **The Crimson Blood Droplet**: The universal symbol of life, cellular vitality, and urgent transfusion need.
2. **The Embracing Heart / Helping Hands**: A protective inner white silhouette forming an anatomical heart contour and supportive human connection.
3. **The Vitality Core**: A central ruby node symbolizing the donor-recipient link.

## 3. Brand Color Palette (Derived from Logo)

### Primary Colors
- **ROKTOS Crimson (Primary)**: `#BE123C` (RGB 190, 18, 60) — Represents life-giving blood, urgency, and passion.
- **Deep Ruby (Primary Dark)**: `#881337` (RGB 136, 19, 55) — Used for dark headers, deep contrast, and active states.
- **Care Rose (Primary Light)**: `#FFE4E6` (RGB 255, 228, 230) — Gentle background tint for blood groups and alert badges.

### Secondary Colors (Trust & Healthcare)
- **Medical Deep Teal (Secondary)**: `#0F766E` (RGB 15, 118, 110) — Balances crimson with calm clinical trust and security.
- **Forest Midnight (Secondary Dark)**: `#134E4A` (RGB 19, 78, 74) — Navbars and primary conversion cards.
- **Mint Accent (Secondary Light)**: `#CCFBF1` (RGB 204, 251, 241) — Verified status badges and success states.

### Neutral & Surface Colors
- **Primary Text**: `#0F172A` (Slate 900) — High contrast readability.
- **Muted Text**: `#475569` (Slate 600) — Secondary labels and captions.
- **Canvas Background**: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) — Warm, clinical white without sterile harshness.
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (White) — Content cards and modals.
- **Borders & Dividers**: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) — Crisp visual containment.

---

## 4. Typography
- **Primary Display & Bengali Text**: `Hind Siliguri`, with fallbacks to `Noto Sans Bengali`, `Kalpurush`, sans-serif.
- **Latin & Numerical Text**: Clean geometric sans-serif (`Inter`, system-ui).
- **Zero-Pill Discipline**: Button corners and card containers feature crisp, purposeful radii (`rounded-md` 6px, `rounded-lg` 8px). Avoid floating bubble aesthetics.
