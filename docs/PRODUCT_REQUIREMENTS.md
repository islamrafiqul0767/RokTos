# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Executive Summary
RoktoBondhu is a non-commercial web application designed for Bangladesh to connect patients needing blood transfusions with willing community donors.

## 2. Core Epics
- **EPIC 1: Public Donor Discovery**: Privacy-preserving directory with zero phone/email exposure in open HTML. Filterable by division, district, blood group, and self-reported availability.
- **EPIC 2: Structured Blood Requests**: Clinical request submission with hospital details and urgency classification.
- **EPIC 3: Consent-First Contact Requests**: Mutual opt-in messaging where donor contact details are released only after donor approves.
- **EPIC 4: Verification Without Paywalls**: Separate Identity and Blood Group review states based on audited verification evidence.
- **EPIC 5: Safety & Medical Compliance**: Prominent disclaimers requiring mandatory on-site cross-matching and infectious disease screening at licensed blood banks.
